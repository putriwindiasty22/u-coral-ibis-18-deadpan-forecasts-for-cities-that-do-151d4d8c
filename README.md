One deadpan weather line for a city that does not exist, delivered like a real
broadcast — the weather in Varn Hollow is ordinary, only the precipitation is
unsent text messages:

> In Varn Hollow, expect a steady drizzle of unsent text messages this evening, a low of 41, and gusts of regret reaching 30 miles per hour after dark.

`main.ts` writes that line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` outside the grader).
